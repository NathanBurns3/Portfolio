About Me

I’m a Full Stack Software Developer at London Computer Systems, where I work on the company’s flagship product, Rent Manager Express, a web-based application for property management.

I graduated from the University of Cincinnati with a B.S. in Software Development, a minor in Computer Science, and an MBA through the University’s Accelerated Program. Before starting full-time, I completed five Software Developer co-ops at London Computer Systems.

I’ve been interested in technology for as long as I can remember. My father worked in IT, so I was introduced to different areas of technology at a young age. I started taking engineering classes when I was 12, but it wasn’t until my sophomore year of high school that I really fell in love with coding. Since then, I’ve continued learning, building, and finding new ways to grow as a developer.
