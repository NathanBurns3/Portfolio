About Me

At the age of 12, I began taking engineering classes, but it wasn't until my sophomore year of high school that I truly fell in love with coding.

I recently completed my fifth and final co-op as a Software Developer Intern at London Computer Systems. In this role, I am contributing to the development of the company's flagship product, Rent Manager Express, a web-based application for property management.
